POLICY REF: GOV-500 - Escalation, LLM-Assisted Review, and Audit Requirements

Where the deterministic rule engine returns an UNCERTAIN verdict on any
individual check, or where aggregate confidence across all checks falls
below the configured threshold, the document must be escalated for
LLM-assisted review rather than defaulted to PASS or FAIL. The LLM reviewer
must (1) explicitly state which rule(s) triggered escalation, (2) ground its
reasoning in the retrieved policy text rather than general knowledge, (3)
produce a plain-language explanation suitable for a non-technical compliance
officer, and (4) record a final PASS/FAIL/UNCERTAIN verdict - UNCERTAIN
should be reserved for cases where even a human reviewer would need
additional source documents to decide. Every review, whether automated or
LLM-assisted, must be linked to an audit trail entry recording the inputs
considered and the verdict rendered, to satisfy downstream regulatory
examination.

POLICY REF: DOC-330 - Document Currency and Expiry Handling

Any identification, certification, or attestation document must carry a date
that is both parseable and not expired at the time of review. Documents dated
more than 18 months in the past for recurring attestations (e.g. annual
compliance certifications) should be treated as stale and routed for review
rather than an automatic FAIL, since renewal paperwork is frequently
back-dated to a prior certification period during legitimate administrative
lag. A document with no discernible date at all is a stronger signal of
non-compliance than a document with a stale-but-present date, and should be
weighted accordingly. When in doubt, the reviewer should recommend requesting
a reissued document rather than issuing a hard rejection.

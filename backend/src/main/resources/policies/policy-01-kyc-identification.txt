POLICY REF: KYC-101 - Customer Identification and Reference Numbering

All customer-facing documents accepted for onboarding or transaction processing
must contain a verifiable account or reference number matching the format
issued by the originating business unit (alphanumeric, 6-14 characters,
optionally hyphenated, e.g. ACC-2024-00931 or REF7788321A). A document lacking
any discernible reference or account identifier cannot be auto-approved and
must be routed for manual or LLM-assisted review to determine whether the
omission is a scanning/OCR artifact or a genuine documentation gap. Reviewers
should note that low-quality scans of otherwise valid documents are the most
common cause of missing-field false positives, and this should be weighed
against the possibility of an intentionally incomplete or fraudulent
submission.

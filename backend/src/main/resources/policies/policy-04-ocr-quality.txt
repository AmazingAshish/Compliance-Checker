POLICY REF: OPS-410 - OCR and Scan Quality Escalation Guidance

Automated extraction of scanned or photographed documents via OCR is
inherently lossy. When a document's extracted text is unusually short (fewer
than ~50 characters), contains a high density of non-alphanumeric noise, or
fails required-field checks in a pattern consistent with a garbled scan
(e.g. fragments of a date or reference number that don't quite parse), this
should be treated as an extraction-quality issue in the first instance rather
than a compliance failure. Reviewers should explicitly consider whether
re-scanning at higher resolution or in better lighting would likely resolve
the ambiguity, and should say so in their reasoning when recommending
UNCERTAIN over an outright FAIL.

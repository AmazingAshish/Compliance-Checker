POLICY REF: ECC-220 - Economic Crime Compliance: Watchlist and High-Risk Terminology

Documents containing terminology historically associated with layering and
concealment in economic crime typologies - including but not limited to
"shell company", "unregistered offshore trust", "anonymous bearer share",
"sanctioned jurisdiction", and "straw director" - must not be auto-approved
regardless of surrounding context. The presence of such a term is not
automatically disqualifying: legitimate legal, audit, and compliance
documents frequently discuss these concepts in an explanatory or advisory
capacity (e.g. a due-diligence memo warning a client away from a shell
company structure). Escalation to a human or LLM reviewer is required to
distinguish between (a) a document describing or warning against a
prohibited structure, and (b) a document that appears to facilitate,
recommend, or represent such a structure as compliant. The reviewer should
quote the surrounding sentence(s) in their reasoning.
